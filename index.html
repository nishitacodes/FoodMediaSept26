Compiling Flavors

Hi, I'm Nishita. As a Bachelor of Culinary Management student at Taylor's University, my approach to food is deeply passionate, focusing heavily on fine dining plating techniques, precise sauce formulations, butchery, and braising methods.

To me, a recipe is an algorithm. Just as code requires perfect syntax to execute efficiently, a dish requires immaculate technique, temperature control, and plating precision. My foodie identity isn't just about eating; it's about understanding the architecture of flavor and the logistics of the kitchen pass.

Inspiration

Gordon Ramsay

Born in Johnstone, Scotland, Ramsay's journey from athlete to multi-Michelin-starred chef exemplifies rigorous discipline. Beyond his intense TV persona, he is a master technician. I am inspired by his relentless standards, precise butchery, and ability to elevate simple ingredients through foundational cooking methods.
